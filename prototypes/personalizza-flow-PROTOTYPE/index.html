Personalizza — curated flow

Prototype, throwaway. Drag the cards with the mouse exactly as on the phone. Swipe a card up to reveal the trash.

The flow

  1. Oggi — long-press the date, or the brush in the bar, to open Personalizza.
  2. Galleria — the saved looks, black around them. Swipe sideways to use one, swipe up to delete, tap to go back to the app wearing it. The last card is Aggiungi nuovo.
  3. Aggiungi — a gallery of starting points rather than a blank form: kinds on top, then curated rows, each with a sentence.
  4. Editor — the page at full size. Outlined zones open one small sheet each. Swiping sideways runs through curated variants. Flavor bottom-left, everything else behind •••.
  5. Aggiungi → App — the first time a look is saved it asks one thing: Abbina all'app (the app takes colour, icon and bar from the page) or Personalizza l'app. Either way the look goes in use.
  6. App — the other half of a look, previewed on Corsi (and on the Home Screen for the icon). Four buttons: Abbinata, Colore, Icona, Barra. Picking anything of its own unpairs it; tapping Abbinata pairs it again. Later, only from ••• › App.

What changes from today's Personalizza

  • Delete moves from the context menu + confirmation dialog to swipe up → trash. With one look left the card only rubber-bands.
  • The 9-tile bento goes. Its tiles become zones on the page (date, greeting, stickers, each section, “Aggiungi sezione”) and one ••• menu for what has no zone (Carta, Barra, Disponi, Rinomina).
  • Appearance + paper collapse into swipeable variants — Naturale, Tinta, Contrasto, Notte, Carta — the way photos get Naturale / B&N / Duotono.
  • Nuovo stile stops being a form sheet and becomes a gallery; picking a tile goes straight into the editor with Annulla / Aggiungi.
  • Tapping the middle card exits into it (Lock Screen), instead of opening the editor. Personalizza is the one way to edit.
  • Editing a saved look gets Annulla back (replaces Ripristina): the editor works on a draft, Fine saves.

Decided

  • Adding always uses the new look. Aggiungi saves it, puts it in use, and drops it into the gallery.
  • The app question is asked once per look, when it is added: pair or customise. After that the choice lives only in the editor's ••• › App, whose Annulla/Fine return to the page editor and are saved by its Fine.
  • Barra moves from the page's ••• menu into App, next to Colore and Icona: they are all the app, not the page.
  • Variants are fixed: Naturale, Tinta, Contrasto, Notte, Carta — the same five for every look.
  • Delete is immediate, with an Annulla toast for four seconds.
